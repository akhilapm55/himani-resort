Our Story

A small heritage retreat, run with care.

Formerly known as Camp Nishani Holidays, Himani Heritage Resorts has welcomed travellers to the spice-scented hills of Coorg for more than a decade.

Reimagined as a heritage retreat, we blend the warmth of a small family-run inn with the comforts of a thoughtfully curated boutique stay. Eight rooms, attentive service, and a strong commitment to slow, local hospitality.

From your morning cup of plantation-grown coffee to the bonfire that closes each day, every detail is shaped by the rhythms of Bhagamandala and the people who call it home.
