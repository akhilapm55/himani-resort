FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things our guests ask most often before they arrive.

What are your check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged subject to availability — just let our front desk know in advance.
How do I reach Himani Heritage from the nearest airport?
Kannur International Airport is the closest, roughly 106 km away. Bengaluru's Kempegowda Airport is a longer but more frequent option. We're happy to arrange a private cab for you — just let us know in advance.
Is parking available?
Yes — free private parking is available on site for all guests. Just drive in; our team will help with luggage on arrival.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation policies vary by season and the rate selected at booking. Please refer to your confirmation email or contact our front desk for specifics.
What's the best time of year to visit Coorg?
October through March is most popular — cool, dry, and clear. The monsoon (June–September) is dramatic and green if you don't mind a little rain, and the waterfalls are at their best.
