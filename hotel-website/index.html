What We Offer

We love to be hospitable.

From plantation walks at sunrise to airport transfers and quiet evenings by the bonfire — we look after every detail of your stay so you can simply relax into the rhythm of Coorg.

More

Plantation Walks

Guided morning walks through working coffee, cardamom, and pepper estates surrounding the resort.

Coorgi Cuisine

Hearty home-style cooking — Pandi curry, akki roti, and fresh local produce — served all day in our dining room.

Wi-Fi & Quiet Corners

High-speed internet throughout the resort and quiet reading corners by the garden — for those who like to mix work with rest.

Airport Transfer

Private cab arrangements from Kannur International Airport (106 km) — let our front desk know in advance.

Experiences

Slow days, simple pleasures

From sunrise yoga to a fresh-roasted cup of estate coffee, the day at Himani Heritage is shaped around the rhythms of the hills.

Plantation Walks & Coffee Tasting
Stroll the surrounding coffee, cardamom, and pepper estates with a local guide, then sample a fresh-brewed cup straight from the bean.
Sunrise Yoga in the Garden
Begin the day with gentle yoga on our garden lawn as the mist lifts from the surrounding hills — instructor available on request.
Traditional Ayurvedic Massage
Restorative Ayurvedic treatments using traditional Kerala-style oils and techniques, arranged in-room on advance request.
Food & Drink

Coorgi flavours, served fresh from the kitchen

Hearty Pandi curry, akki roti hot off the tava, kadambuttu, and pots of estate-roasted coffee — our kitchen serves home-style Coorg cuisine alongside familiar comfort food for younger guests and travellers.

Just beyond the gate

Triveni Sangama — the confluence at our doorstep

The meeting of the Kaveri, Kanika, and the mythical Sujyothi rivers — a short walk from the resort and a quiet spot at sunrise.

Abbi Falls — a hidden cascade in the hills

A short drive, then a gentle walk through coffee estates to one of Coorg's most beautiful waterfalls. Best in monsoon.
